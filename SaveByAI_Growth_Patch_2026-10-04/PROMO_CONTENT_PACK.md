# SaveByAI — free promotion pack

## Channel priority for India
1. Instagram Reels
2. YouTube Shorts
3. Facebook Reels (reuse the same vertical video)
4. Brand posts in relevant communities only where self-promotion is permitted

TikTok is not a primary India channel because access remains restricted in India. Do not spend time building an India acquisition plan around it.

## Posting rule
Every video should teach one useful thing first. CTA comes at the end:

**"Check before you buy — SaveByAI.in"**

Do not claim a live cashback rate until verified.

## Reel / Short 1 — The price is not the real price
**Hook:** "A ₹60,000 laptop may not really cost ₹60,000."

On-screen sequence:
- Listed price: ₹60,000
- Coupon: -₹2,000
- Card offer: -₹3,000
- Gift-card saving: -₹1,000
- Eligible reward: -₹1,200
- Effective price: ₹52,800

Voice/text: "The trick is to compare the effective price, not the banner price. SaveByAI is building one place to do that."

CTA: `savebyai.in`

## Reel / Short 2 — 4 checks before buying online
Hook: "Before you click Buy Now in India, check these four things."
1. Coupon
2. Bank/card offer
3. Gift-card discount
4. Eligible cashback

End: "Not every offer stacks. Calculate the real price first."

## Reel / Short 3 — Cashback is not always cashback
Hook: "Affiliate commission does NOT automatically mean you get cashback."
Explain in 15–20 sec that customer rewards need to be permitted and tracked.
CTA: "SaveByAI will only show verified customer-facing routes."

## Reel / Short 4 — Myntra checklist
Hook: "Buying on Myntra? Don't stop at the sale price."
Show: cart price → coupon → card cap → gift card → eligible cashback.
CTA to the Myntra guide on SaveByAI.

## Reel / Short 5 — Amazon checklist
Hook: "Amazon's lowest visible price may not be your lowest payable price."
Show: seller → coupon → card cap → exchange → rewards → compare other retailers.

## Reel / Short 6 — Travel deals
Hook: "₹1,000 off can still be a worse travel deal."
Show: headline fare + convenience fee + cancellation terms + bank offer = compare final trip cost.

## Reel / Short 7 — Laptop buying route
Hook: "Brand website or Amazon/Flipkart — where should you buy a laptop?"
Show: same spec, warranty, card offer, exchange, effective price.

## Reel / Short 8 — iPhone route
Hook: "Before buying an iPhone, compare these 5 numbers."
Retail price, card offer, trade-in, gift-card saving, eligible cashback.

## Reel / Short 9 — Why SaveByAI exists
Hook: "Coupons are everywhere. Cashback is somewhere else. Bank offers are hidden in the terms."
End: "SaveByAI is trying to combine the route into one effective price."

## Reel / Short 10 — Ask the audience
Hook: "Which Indian store should SaveByAI add first?"
Options on screen: Amazon / Myntra / Flipkart / MakeMyTrip / Nykaa / Other
CTA: "Search it on SaveByAI.in or join early access and tell us."
