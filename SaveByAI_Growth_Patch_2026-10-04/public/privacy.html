Privacy notice

SaveByAI is an early-stage savings and cashback service. We do not ask for or store your merchant payment-card details.

Cashback tracking

When you activate cashback, we may store the email address you provide, a SaveByAI user identifier, a unique affiliate click identifier, the merchant, time of click, cashback label/rate active at that time, basic source information and country. We use this information to associate eligible affiliate transactions with your cashback record and to send essential cashback-status messages.

You do not need to provide an email merely to browse SaveByAI. If you continue to a merchant without cashback, we may still record an anonymous click identifier so the partner route can be measured. Recent trip IDs may also be stored locally in your browser to support the Claim Cashback feature.

Cashback claims

If you submit a claim, we store the email, merchant, optional order reference, purchase date, notes and any SaveByAI click ID available on your device. Claims without a matching tracked click may require manual review and may not be recoverable from the affiliate network.

Analytics

We use lightweight product events to understand which pages, searches, merchants and features are useful. We avoid putting email addresses or arbitrary personal text inside analytics event properties.

Early-access marketing

The early-access form on the homepage is separate from cashback tracking. If you opt in there, we store your email and optional interest to send SaveByAI product and savings updates. You can opt out of those marketing messages.

Hosting and infrastructure

The site uses Cloudflare services for hosting, database, security and operational processing. Affiliate networks and merchants process their own tracking and purchase data under their respective policies.

Your choices

You can ask us about or request deletion of SaveByAI-held personal data by emailing savebyai@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, disputes or legal obligations.
