Privacy notice — early access

SaveByAI does not currently create customer accounts or store payment-card details.

Analytics

We use lightweight website analytics and anonymous product events to understand which pages, searches, merchants and calculator features are useful. We avoid storing the text of arbitrary personal information in analytics events.

Early-access email

If you voluntarily join early access, we store the email address you submit, the optional merchant/product interest you provide, the submission time, and basic source information needed to understand how people found SaveByAI. We use this only for SaveByAI early-access updates and relevant product/savings notifications.

Hosting and infrastructure

The site is hosted using Cloudflare services. Hosting, security and operational logs may be processed by Cloudflare as part of providing the service.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your early-access email or stop contacting you by emailing savebyai@gmail.com. We will update this notice before adding customer accounts, payments, wallets or materially different tracking.
