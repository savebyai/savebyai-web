LAPTOP BUYING GUIDE

Buying a laptop in India: compare the whole route.

The cheapest listing is not always the best deal once RAM/storage configuration, warranty, bank offers and exchange are included.

1. Lock the exact specification

Compare the same processor, RAM, storage, display and warranty. Small configuration differences can make price comparisons misleading.

2. Check the brand store

Lenovo, HP, Dell and other manufacturers may have direct-store offers, bundles or support differences versus marketplaces.

3. Check bank and EMI conditions

Separate instant discount from no-cost EMI and read processing-fee or tenure conditions.

4. Treat exchange as personal value

Exchange can be useful, but it depends on your old device and may not be comparable across retailers.

5. Include warranty and add-ons

A lower price with weaker warranty or a different operating-system licence may not be equivalent.

6. Calculate effective price

Use only discounts you are definitely eligible for. Then compare that number across retailers.
