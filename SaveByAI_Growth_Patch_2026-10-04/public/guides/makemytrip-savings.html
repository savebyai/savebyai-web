TRAVEL SAVINGS GUIDE

Travel deals: compare the final trip cost, not the banner.

A travel coupon can look strong while convenience fees, fare rules or cancellation conditions make another route better.

1. Compare the same itinerary

Match flight, baggage, hotel room type, dates and cancellation conditions before comparing prices.

2. Include taxes and convenience fees

Use the final payable amount at checkout. Headline search results can exclude fees that appear later.

3. Check coupon conditions

Look for minimum booking value, payment method, route restrictions, user type and maximum discount cap.

4. Check bank offers separately

Some card offers cannot combine with a coupon. Compare both routes rather than automatically adding both savings.

5. Value flexibility

A slightly higher price can be better if cancellation or date-change terms matter to you.

6. Compare direct booking

For hotels and some flights, compare the direct supplier price as well as marketplace routes.
