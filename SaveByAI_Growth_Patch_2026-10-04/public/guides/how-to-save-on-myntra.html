MYNTRA SAVINGS GUIDE

How to save on Myntra without double-counting discounts.

The cheapest-looking headline discount is not always the lowest final cost. Compare the amount you actually pay after every condition that genuinely applies to your order.

1. Start with the final cart price

Choose the same size, seller and delivery option before comparing routes. A percentage banner is less useful than the rupee amount at checkout.

2. Check coupon eligibility

Look at minimum order value, eligible brands, new-user restrictions and whether the coupon blocks another promotion.

3. Check card or UPI offers

Record the maximum discount cap and payment-method requirement. Do not assume a bank offer stacks with every coupon.

4. Treat gift-card savings separately

If you can legitimately buy a gift card below face value, compare the saving only after confirming it can be used for the same order and does not invalidate other offers.

5. Add cashback only when eligible

Cashback can have category, new-customer, tracking and validation rules. Count it only if the route explicitly allows your purchase.

6. Compare the effective price

Listed/cart price minus genuine coupon, gift-card, card and eligible cashback savings gives you a cleaner comparison.

Important: SaveByAI is not currently claiming a live Myntra cashback rate. This guide explains the method we use while partner routes are being verified.
