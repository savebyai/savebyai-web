IPHONE BUYING GUIDE

Buying an iPhone in India: find the best legitimate route.

iPhone pricing often differs by retailer, card, trade-in and sale period. Compare the final effective cost while protecting warranty and return rights.

1. Compare the exact model and storage

Match model, storage, colour only if it changes price, and whether the device is sold by an authorised retailer.

2. Check instant card savings

Use the actual eligible amount after card type, minimum value and maximum cap are applied.

3. Compare trade-in independently

Trade-in is specific to your existing phone and its assessed condition. Keep it separate from universal discounts.

4. Check gift-card routes carefully

If a legitimate discounted gift card can fund the purchase, confirm payment compatibility and any denomination limits.

5. Add cashback only if tracking is allowed

Do not count an affiliate reward when the product category, coupon or payment route would invalidate it.

6. Protect warranty and returns

A small saving is not worth losing important purchase protection. Compare authorised channels and invoice terms.
