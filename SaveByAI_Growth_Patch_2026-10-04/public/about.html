ABOUT SAVEBYAI

Help households keep more of the money they already earn.

SaveByAI is being built around a simple idea: every recurring expense or planned purchase should be checked for a genuinely better option.

What we are building

SaveByAI starts by identifying areas of household spending that may be worth reviewing. Over time, the aim is to bring more savings mechanisms into one place — reducing waste, switching where it makes sense, choosing a better payment route, using legitimate offers and tracking cashback where available.

OUR RULE

Customer saving first. Our commission second.

If the option that saves you the most earns SaveByAI nothing, that should still be the recommendation.

How SaveByAI may earn money

Some shopping partners may pay SaveByAI a commission when an eligible purchase is made through a tracked partner route. Where customer cashback is offered, part of that commission may be shared with the customer. Affiliate relationships are disclosed and should not determine which option is presented as the best saving route.

Where the product is today

SaveByAI is an early beta. The Household Savings Check is intentionally narrow, and personalised reviews for early users may be prepared individually while we learn which savings problems are genuinely useful to solve.
