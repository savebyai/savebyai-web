HOW IT WORKS

Cashback without a heavy signup journey.

Browse freely. We only ask for an email at the moment you activate an eligible cashback offer.

01

Choose a store

Search SaveByAI and open a live merchant card. No account required to browse.

02

Activate & shop

For eligible cashback, enter your email in a small popup. We create a silent identity and unique click ID, then send you to the merchant.

03

We match the purchase

When the affiliate network reports the sale against that click ID, we can associate it with your cashback record.

04

Claim if something was missed

If you shopped anonymously or cashback did not attach, use Claim Cashback and we will try to match your tracked trip.
