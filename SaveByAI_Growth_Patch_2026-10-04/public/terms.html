SaveByAI MVP terms

SaveByAI is currently a validation-stage service. Merchant prices, partner programmes, attribution rules, eligible products and cashback rates can change.

Cashback eligibility

Cashback is only offered where SaveByAI explicitly displays an eligible cashback offer. A purchase must normally begin through the relevant SaveByAI tracked partner route and satisfy the merchant/affiliate programme rules. Cashback is not guaranteed merely because a user visited a merchant.

Status and confirmation

A tracked or claimed purchase may remain pending while the advertiser validates it. Returns, cancellations, ineligible products, excluded payment methods, another affiliate becoming the last qualifying referrer, tracking prevention or other programme exclusions can cause a transaction to be declined. Cashback becomes payable only after the qualifying transaction is confirmed and the associated commission is confirmed/earned by SaveByAI.

Claim Cashback

The claim form is a recovery and review process, not a way to create affiliate tracking after a purchase. If there was no eligible tracked SaveByAI click before purchase, SaveByAI may be unable to recover or pay cashback.

Rates and estimates

Where a rate is described as “up to”, the actual reward may depend on customer type, product/category, order value and other advertiser rules. The effective-price calculator uses values entered by the user and does not guarantee that different offers can be combined.

MVP fulfilment

During early validation, confirmed cashback may be processed manually while automated wallet and payout features are being developed. SaveByAI will not ask you to provide merchant card details for cashback tracking.
